# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Description

The AI-Powered Coaching Academy Growth & Admissions Platform is a centralized platform designed to help coaching institutes improve their marketing activities, generate student inquiries, manage admission processes and measure business performance.

The platform aims to address common challenges faced by coaching institutes, including continuous student enrollment pressure, time-consuming content creation, inefficient inquiry management, inconsistent follow-ups and difficulty measuring the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

It combines AI-powered content generation, admission management, marketing analytics, student success story management and communication automation into a unified platform.

The primary objective is to help coaching institutes attract prospective students, convert inquiries into admissions and make data-driven business decisions.

## 1.2 Vision Statement

To become a growth-focused digital platform that helps coaching institutes simplify marketing, improve student acquisition and manage their admission journey more effectively through AI and automation.

## 1.3 Product Mission

To reduce the manual effort involved in marketing and admissions while providing coaching institutes with actionable insights into their business growth.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Coaching institutes operate in a competitive environment where attracting new students and maintaining consistent enrollment are essential for business sustainability.

However, many institutes face challenges in managing their marketing and admission activities.

The major problems identified are:

1. **Time-consuming content creation:** Institutes regularly need social media posts, promotional videos, captions, advertisements and educational content to attract students.

2. **Inefficient inquiry management:** Student inquiries arrive through multiple channels, making it difficult to maintain organized records.

3. **Missed follow-ups:** Admission counsellors may forget to contact prospective students, resulting in lost admission opportunities.

4. **Lack of marketing performance visibility:** Institutes often struggle to identify which campaigns generate actual admissions.

5. **Underutilization of student achievements:** Previous student results, testimonials and success stories are not always effectively used in marketing activities.

6. **Manual communication:** Sending admission reminders, demo class notifications and promotional messages requires repetitive manual effort.

7. **Limited business insights:** Institute owners may lack a centralized view of inquiries, conversions, campaign performance and admission trends.

The proposed platform aims to solve these problems through a unified growth and admissions management experience.

# 5. Product Features and Functional Requirements

## 5.1 AI Marketing & Content Studio

**Description:**

An AI-powered workspace that helps coaching institutes generate marketing and educational content using their existing course information, teaching materials and student achievements.

**Functional Requirements:**

- Generate social media captions and post descriptions.
- Generate Instagram and Facebook promotional content.
- Create short-form video and Instagram Reel scripts.
- Generate WhatsApp promotional messages.
- Create course announcement and admission campaign content.
- Generate content ideas based on examination categories.
- Generate content based on previous student achievements.
- Convert educational material into multiple content formats.
- Generate content calendars for planned marketing activities.
- Allow users to edit and refine AI-generated content.
- Save generated content for future use.
- Organize content by course, examination and campaign.

**Expected Outcome:**

Reduce the time and effort required to create consistent marketing content.

## 5.2 Smart Admission CRM

**Description:**

A centralized inquiry and admission management system that allows institutes to organize prospective student information and monitor their admission journey.

**Functional Requirements:**

- Capture student inquiries through online inquiry forms.
- Maintain student inquiry profiles.
- Record student name and contact information.
- Record interested courses and examinations.
- Track the source of each inquiry.
- Assign inquiries to admission counsellors.
- Maintain inquiry history.
- Track the current status of every inquiry.
- Schedule and record follow-ups.
- Add notes to individual inquiry profiles.
- Identify inquiries requiring follow-up.
- Track successful and unsuccessful admissions.
- Convert interested students into confirmed admissions.

**Admission Status Categories:**

- New Inquiry.
- Contacted.
- Interested.
- Demo Class Scheduled.
- Demo Completed.
- Admission Pending.
- Admission Confirmed.
- Not Interested.

**Expected Outcome:**

Provide a structured admission process and minimize the possibility of losing prospective students due to missed inquiries or follow-ups.

## 5.3 Marketing Analytics & Business Dashboard

**Description:**

A centralized dashboard that provides institute owners with insights into marketing activities, student inquiries, admissions and business performance.

**Functional Requirements:**

- Display total inquiries received.
- Display total confirmed admissions.
- Calculate inquiry-to-admission conversion rates.
- Display inquiries based on their sources.
- Compare campaign performance.
- Track marketing expenditure.
- Calculate cost per inquiry.
- Calculate cost per admission.
- Display admission trends over selected periods.
- Track revenue attributed to marketing campaigns where reliable data is available.
- Provide visual reports and performance summaries.
- Allow filtering by course, campaign and date range.

**Expected Outcome:**

Help institute owners understand marketing effectiveness and make better business decisions using measurable information.

# 6. User Roles, Permissions and Employee Management

The platform supports dedicated user roles to provide appropriate operational access while enforcing strict institute-level data isolation.

| User Role | Responsibilities & Access Scope |
|---|---|
| Platform Super Admin | Global platform oversight, cross-tenant system metrics, platform billing health |
| Institute Owner | Commercial owner; manages billing, institute profile, employee invitations, role assignment, and executive analytics |
| Institute Administrator | Operations manager; manages inquiries, staff assignments, courses, campaigns, and approves testimonials |
| Admission Counsellor | Front-line consultant; manages assigned inquiries, schedules demos, logs calls/notes, and records confirmed admissions |
| Marketing Manager | Growth specialist; creates and edits AI promotional copy, manages ad campaigns, logs spend, and tracks CPI/CPA |
| Teacher / Faculty | Academic instructor; submits study notes for AI repurposing and uploads verified student test results |

### 6.1 Employee Account Management Workflow

Institute owners can invite and govern their team members through the Employee Management console:
1. **Owner Registration:** Owner completes self-service registration, verifies their email, and sets up the institute workspace.
2. **Employee Invitation:** Owner opens Employee Management (`/settings/team`) and enters an employee's email address and assigned role.
3. **Invitation Delivery:** A time-limited invitation token (valid for 7 days) is generated and sent to the employee.
4. **Credential Setup:** The employee opens their invitation link, creates their personal password, and configures their profile.
5. **Role-Restricted Access:** The employee logs in using their individual credentials and is granted access exclusively to their institute's data within the boundaries of their assigned role.

# 7. Key User Workflows

## 7.0 Institute Registration, Onboarding & Trial Activation Workflow

1. Institute owner signs up via the public registration screen (`/signup`).
2. Supabase Auth dispatches a verification email.
3. Owner confirms their email address and is directed to the Institute Onboarding Wizard.
4. Owner submits institute legal name, vanity slug (e.g. `apex-academy`), contact phone, city, state, and optional logo.
5. The system atomically provisions the institute, binds the owner as `OWNER`, and activates a 14-day free trial subscription (`status = 'TRIAL'`).
6. Owner is redirected into their personalized Institute Dashboard.

## 7.1 Marketing Content Generation Workflow

1. User logs into the platform.
2. User selects the AI Marketing Studio.
3. User chooses the examination or course.
4. User provides relevant information or uploads existing educational material.
5. AI generates suitable marketing content.
6. User reviews and edits the generated content.
7. User saves the content for use in marketing campaigns.

## 7.2 Student Inquiry to Admission Workflow

1. A prospective student visits an institute's inquiry page.
2. The student submits an inquiry.
3. The inquiry is recorded in the platform.
4. The inquiry is assigned to an admission counsellor.
5. The counsellor contacts the student.
6. The counsellor updates the inquiry status.
7. Follow-up reminders are maintained.
8. The student attends a demo class if applicable.
9. The counsellor records the admission outcome.
10. Confirmed admissions are reflected in the dashboard.

## 7.3 Student Success Story Workflow

1. Institute uploads student achievement information.
2. The information is reviewed and verified.
3. Required student or parent consent is obtained.
4. The user selects a promotional content format.
5. AI generates a success story or promotional caption.
6. The user reviews and approves the content.
7. The content is saved for marketing use.

## 7.4 Marketing Performance Workflow

1. Institute creates or records a marketing campaign.
2. Campaign information and expenditure are maintained.
3. Prospective students submit inquiries through campaign-specific sources.
4. The platform associates inquiries with their sources.
5. Admission outcomes are updated.
6. The dashboard displays campaign performance.
7. Institute owners compare campaigns and evaluate their effectiveness.

# 9. Non-Functional Product Requirements

The platform should satisfy the following quality expectations:

**Usability**
- The interface should be simple and understandable.
- Users should be able to access important functions without extensive training.
- The platform should provide clear navigation.

**Performance**
- Dashboard information should load efficiently.
- AI content generation should provide clear processing and completion feedback.
- Large inquiry records should remain manageable.

**Security and Privacy**
- Institute information should be protected.
- Users should access only the information permitted by their roles.
- Student contact details should be handled responsibly.
- Student achievements and testimonials should require appropriate consent.
- Marketing communication should follow applicable consent and messaging regulations.

**Scalability**
- The product should support multiple coaching institutes.
- Each institute should have its own separate business information.
- Additional users, courses and campaigns should be manageable as an institute grows.

**Reliability**
- Inquiry and admission records should remain accurate.
- Important changes should be traceable.
- Reports should use consistent and reliable data.

# 12. Assumptions and Constraints

## Assumptions

1. Coaching institutes have recurring student admission requirements.
2. Institutes are interested in improving their marketing activities.
3. Educational content and student achievements can be reused for promotional purposes with appropriate permissions.
4. Institute staff can use a centralized digital platform.
5. Institutes are willing to adopt a subscription-based solution if it demonstrates measurable value.

## Constraints

1. AI-generated content may require manual verification and editing.
2. Marketing campaign attribution depends on accurate inquiry source tracking.
3. WhatsApp automation depends on approved external business messaging services.
4. AI-generated student success stories must be based on verified information.
5. Business insights will depend on the quality and completeness of institute data.
6. The initial product will prioritize marketing and admissions rather than attempting to replace every existing coaching management system.
